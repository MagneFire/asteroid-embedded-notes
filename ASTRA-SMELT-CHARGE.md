1. **Thermal protection misses its advertised deadline. READ:** [Polling]( /Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-smelt2/src/soc/gauge_max17050.c:104) is throttled to 30 s; the charger task is lazy, and sleep normally relies on the 60 s stats wake. Dock interrupts do not force a temperature check. **INFER:** charging remains allowed after temperature rises or the gauge fails, until another check runs. A sampled TEMP failure stops charging; an absent gauge leaves GPIO 53 high. Give charging protection its own wake deadline.

2. **Dock feedback can undo termination. READ:** [Policy]( /Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-smelt2/src/soc/charge_policy.c:24) clears `complete` on an undocked sample after five seconds; redocking resets the warm-cycle timer. Its debounce is evaluated only every 30–60 s. **INFER:** if termination holds GPIO 54 high long enough, completion can clear and charging restart without reaching 98%; repeated transitions can restart warm on-periods. The hot latch itself survives. Raw GPIO 54 also controls MPM arming outside this debounce: transient wake bursts are plausible, a persistent storm is unproved.

3. **“Temperature valid” means only “I²C succeeded.” READ:** [Integration]( /Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-smelt2/src/soc/gauge_max17050.c:48) ignores no-battery/POR status for permission, retains stale SOC after read failures, and accepts any returned TEMP word. **INFER:** a responding but invalid/reset gauge or failed sensor can supply an apparently cool value and enable charging. This is narrower than fail-closed handling of unknown battery state.

4. **Hard shutdown and 60 °C lack board validation. READ:** `pmic_shutdown_mode()` selects shutdown type `0x4`; `reboot()` subsequently drops PS_HOLD, but first flushes settings and falls back to a watchdog reset. PMIC writes discard errors. The supplied vendor evidence establishes 53 °C termination, not 60 °C shutdown. **INFER:** this is an intended shutdown sequence, not proof of durable power-off on the dock; receiver power, GPIO retention and charger-triggered restart could defeat it or cause repeated boots. SSH to the kernel source was sandbox-blocked. Do not treat 60 °C as a validated safe limit.

5. **Reduced-rate control is missing. READ:** local `healthd` disassembly at `0x28f28–0x28fd0` enables `chg_rate=1` above **4100 mV**, retaining it until voltage falls to **3900 mV or below**. The patch never controls GPIO 58. **INFER:** charging inherits an unspecified rate and can omit vendor reduction near full—the first-boot overheating condition. Explicitly implement that voltage hysteresis.

6. **Completion is not equivalent to sustained charging taper. READ:** [Completion logic]( /Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-smelt2/src/soc/charge_policy.c:43) counts failed current reads as zero, accepts small discharge currents, preserves its count across 99%, and starts its timeout before reaching 100%. It also counts while thermally stopped. Positive-discharge conversion is consistent; the symmetric ±16 mA test masks direction rather than verifying taper. Vendor code starts its timer upon reaching full and rejects failed current reads. These differences can declare completion prematurely.

7. **Time wrap corrupts cycle timing. READ:** `time_ms()/1000` wraps after approximately 49.7 days, but policy subtraction assumes a full 32-bit *seconds* wrap. **INFER:** warm off-time can end prematurely and completion timeouts can fire immediately. This does not override a sampled hot/invalid-temperature stop.

8. **Initial GPIO assertion can briefly enable charging. READ:** [Initialization]( /Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-smelt2/src/soc/gauge_max17050.c:81) enables output before loading high, and runs after display initialization. **INFER:** an inherited low output latch can produce a low pulse; earlier boot failures leave charging uncontrolled. Preload high before enabling output.

**Short supervised boot checks:** from cool, verify GPIO 53 high actually stops charge current; observe GPIO 54/94 and wake counts through termination/release; confirm GPIO 53 survives sleep and temperature checks occur on schedule. Check GPIO 58 and voltage/current together. Validate shutdown/restart behavior using a cool forced-shutdown test—not by heating to 60 °C.
