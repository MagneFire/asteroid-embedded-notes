The core is usable, but the idle and service contracts need work before replacing the loop.

1. **High — idle handoff remains racy.** [spm.c:143](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/soc/spm.c:143) falls back to unmasked WFI; its collapse path masks IRQs without checking scheduler work. [frame.c:218](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/frame.c:218) also sleeps unmasked. An IRQ can post, return, then leave software work asleep. WFI can meet the requirement with IRQ-save, a fresh pending/deadline check, timer programming, WFI while masked, and IRQ-restore. Collapse additionally depends on secure firmware rejecting pending hardware wakes across the SMC; this repository cannot prove that. Cover fallback and bailout paths too.

2. **High — IRQ-context deadline mutation is unsafe.** [sched.c:32](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/sched.c:32) writes `at`/`armed` without synchronization. An IRQ rearming between `due()` and [the disarm at :59](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/sched.c:59) loses its deadline; concurrent task/IRQ updates can also overwrite one another. Task-side `sched_at` interrupted by an IRQ that **only posts** is safe. Specify deadline operations as main-context-only, or protect all mutation/consumption and define competing-rearm semantics. IRQ arming must also invalidate an already calculated sleep interval.

3. **High — preserve restricted nested pumps.** [display.c:33](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/display/display.c:33) pumps USB inside `pp_wait`; replacing that hook with `sched_run` permits recursive rendering, power transitions and USB dispatch. Add an explicit non-reentrancy contract. Likewise, [npl.c:182](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/bt/npl.c:182) needs synchronous HCI progress during semaphore waits: posting the blocked BT task cannot provide it. Keep that transport pump; bridge callout reset/stop to the earliest BT deadline and repost partially drained event queues.

4. **High — deadlines alone cannot replace power coordination.** [power.c:247](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/power.c:247) combines USB progress, clock boosting, blockers, cluster eligibility, link parking and restoration. `usb_busy()` includes transfers in flight, so it is not simply “runnable now.” Preserve these distinctions in an idle coordinator. After cluster wake, explicitly dispatch or conservatively post wake consumers: [spm.c:98](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/soc/spm.c:98) has an empty MPM handler; UART/touch/PMIC task posts are not guaranteed merely because the cluster resumed. Preserve ambient-minute scheduling and recompute it after RTC changes.

5. **Medium — migration order is reasonable, conditionally.** Keep polling unmigrated services. Settings/weather need mutation-triggered scheduling and presenter posts; notifications need RTC snooze deadlines. Charger/BMS need charger recovery deadlines, the rising-edge-only cable check, and the deliberate opportunistic asleep BMS policy ([app.c:323](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/app.c:323)). **Touch is the first structurally unsafe IRQ-only conversion:** [touch_it7260.c:304](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/input/touch_it7260.c:304) recovers missed edges through polling; mode retries and I²C timeout progress also require deadlines. Hub, BT and USB should remain later.

Remove its internal WFI; schedule frame/UI deadlines centrally alongside inactivity, haptics, game and console work before dropping the tick.

7. **Medium — watchdog coverage is incomplete.** [sched.c:97](/Users/darrel/Downloads/AsteroidOS/asteroid-embedded-sched/src/sched.c:97) ignores event-only backlog and forgets lateness once execution disarms a task. It cannot establish service liveness. Also refresh time after slow callbacks and document the half-range deadline limit.
