**1. Counter behaviour and a clean measurement.** Linux returns **software accumulated µAh + the current raw shadow subtotal**, applying gain, rsense and temperature/revision compensation. SOC calculations bank that subtotal and reset the hardware counter; ordinary resets preserve the total, while exceptional paths such as charge completion reset both. Thus raw `0x40a8` endpoint subtraction across Linux resets is invalid. **My leading hypothesis is offset error during cooling**, rather than a broken software accumulator: the negative intervals also have negative instantaneous current. Calibration writes a single offset conversion into hardware; it runs through delayed work and after >5°C PMIC-die changes. Reading the property can itself trigger recalibration, after capturing raw charge. Compensation rescales charge; it does not repair historical offset error. The log cannot distinguish these effects conclusively, and its battery temperature is not PMIC-die temperature.
